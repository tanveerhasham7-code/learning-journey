## What you will learn
- Splunk architecture, deployment options, and lab safety.
- Forwarders, data onboarding, indexes, sourcetypes, fields, and timestamps.
- SPL fundamentals and common search commands.
- CIM, data models, and technology add-ons.
- Authentication, endpoint, process, and network monitoring.
- Detection specifications, alerts, reports, and dashboards.
- Threat hunting, Splunk ES concepts, and risk-based alerting.
- SOC triage, incident response, platform security, and evidence-based reporting.

## Study method
Study each lesson in order. Reproduce queries only when the required data and fields exist. Record query purpose, time range, output, and limitations. Use an authorized lab or synthetic data. Save sanitized evidence in the companion project.

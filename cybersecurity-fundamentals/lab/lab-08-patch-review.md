## Why patch management matters

A patch may fix a security vulnerability, correct a software defect, or improve stability. Delayed security updates can leave known weaknesses available to attackers. Updates should be obtained through trusted vendor or operating-system channels, and important data should be backed up before higher-impact maintenance.

## Safe review procedure for a fresh check

1. Open **Settings → Windows Update**.
2. Select **Check for updates** and wait for the check to finish.
3. Record the exact status message and the date/time of the check.
4. If updates are listed, record their names or KB identifiers and whether they are pending, downloading, installing, or awaiting restart.
5. Review **Update history** if installation details are needed.
6. Do not share serial numbers, account details, or other personal information in screenshots.
7. For third-party applications, use each vendor's built-in updater or official website; do not download patches from unfamiliar pop-ups or links.

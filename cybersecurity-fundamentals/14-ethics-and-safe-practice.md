# 14 — Professional Ethics and Safe Practice

## Authorization and scope
Only access, test, scan, or monitor systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. Permission should identify targets, dates, methods, limits, contacts, and reporting. A learning goal does not create authorization.

## Responsible conduct
Protect privacy, collect only necessary information, avoid disruption, preserve evidence, report findings accurately, and disclose weaknesses through approved channels. Never go beyond the agreed scope.

## Responsible disclosure
Document the affected product/version, safe reproduction evidence, potential impact, and remediation suggestions. Contact the owner through its security reporting process. Do not publish secrets or weaponized details.

## Professional integrity
Never claim a lab, scan, detection, or investigation is complete if it was only planned or described. Separate facts, assumptions, and hypotheses. Keep timestamps, evidence, and conclusions consistent.

## Practical
For a fictional lab, write rules of engagement: authorized targets, excluded targets, permitted methods, test window, safety limits, evidence rules, emergency stop condition, and reporting contact. Keep credentials and private infrastructure details out of GitHub.

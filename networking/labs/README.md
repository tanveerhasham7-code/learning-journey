# Networking Labs

This folder contains the detailed reports and evidence for completed and ongoing hands-on networking labs.

## Lab reports

| Lab | Practical work | Report | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab 01 | Build and verify a basic switched LAN (2 PCs, 1 switch) | [Lab 01 report](lab-01-basic-lan.md) | [Lab 01 screenshots](screenshots/lab-01/) |
| Lab 02 | Connect two separate LANs through a Cisco 2911 router | [Lab 02 report](lab-02-two-lans-router.md) | [Lab 02 screenshots](screenshots/lab-02/) |

## Folder structure

- `lab-01-basic-lan.md` — Lab 1 explanation, configuration, results and screenshots.
- `lab-02-two-lans-router.md` — Lab 2 explanation, router configuration, ping results and screenshots.
- `screenshots/lab-01/` — Lab 1 visual evidence.
- `screenshots/lab-02/` — Lab 2 visual evidence.

## Important

Each report records the actual results visible in the saved evidence. A successful ping in one direction does not automatically prove a zero-loss result in the reverse direction. Keep screenshots in the matching lab folder and update the report whenever new evidence is added.
